YMCA Youth and Government

    Format: In person
    Grade requirements: 9th-12th grade
    Other requirements: $2500 payment (depends on the delegation. Scholarships and aid available)
    Application period: No aplication
    Duration of program: six months (September through February)
    Summary: This program incentivizes students to pursue public policy

Junior Defenders Program

    Format: In person
    Grade Requirements: 9th-12th
    Other requirements: 15-minute interview
    Application period (2 sessions in the summer and one in the winter): April 1 - May 30, October 1 - November 30th
    Sessions: June 22-26, August 3-7, and last week of LAUSD winter break
    Summary: A week-long program at the Clara Shortridge Foltz Criminal Justice Center. This program offers students an overview of court procedures in criminal cases, an overview of the job Public Defenders do, and an opportunity to see real court procedures.

Olivia Mitchell LA City Youth Council

    Format: In person
    Age Requirements: 16-25 years old
    Application period: Due June 30th 2026 (oppening date varies)
    Duration of program: 1 year (starting in September)
    Summary: Students learn about working in local government, participate and host community and civic events, make policies, and help advocate city budget.
    Stipend: $300 per month

Leadership in Action - Kizuna

    Format: In person
    Age requirements: 13-18
    Application period: September 9- November 29
    Duration: 10 Saturdays (January- March)
    Summary: Encourages students to learn and advocate for Japanese Americans. Learning about their marginalization and struggles they have faced. This program encourages leadership, public speaking skills, advocacy, and activism in one’s community.
    Stipend: Stipend available

ACLU Youth Leadership Squad

    Format: In person
    Grade requirements: High School Students
    Application period: Opens in August and closes September 13
    Duration: Aligns with the school year (1 saturday per month)
    Summary: The program centers on civil rights and educational equity in California public schools. Students work alongside ACLU SoCal staff to build organizing and advocacy skills, including storytelling, digital campaigns, policy advocacy, and "Know Your Rights" workshops
    Stipend: $550 upon completion
